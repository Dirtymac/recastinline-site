Recast Inline

Rephrase your selected text in any app — with a single hotkey. Runs entirely on-device with Apple Intelligence. No accounts, no cloud, nothing leaves your Mac.

Download for macOS

Free · Developer ID-signed & notarized · always the latest release

Requirements

  • macOS 26 or later.
  • An Apple Intelligence–capable Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on (the rephrasing runs on the built-in on-device model).

Install

  1. Open the downloaded RecastInline.dmg and drag Recast Inline into your Applications folder.
  2. Double-click Recast Inline to launch it. It's signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally — no security warnings.
  3. When prompted, grant Accessibility permission (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility). This is required for the global hotkey to type the rephrased text back into your app.

How to use it

  1. Select some text in any app.
  2. Press ⌥⌘R — the default shortcut (change it anytime in the app's window), or click the menu-bar R and choose Recast Selection.
  3. Recast Inline rewrites your selection in place and restores your clipboard. A small pulse by the cursor shows it's working.
