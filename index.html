How it works

1

Select your text

Highlight anything in any app — an email draft, a Slack message, a paragraph in a doc.

2

Hit the shortcut

Press ⌥⌘R (change it anytime in Settings), or click the menu-bar R and choose Recast Selection.

3

It rewrites in place

Recast Inline replaces your selection with the polished version — no tab-switching, no clipboard.

Get started

Requirements

  • macOS 26 or later.
  • An Apple Intelligence–capable Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on — the rewriting runs on the built-in on-device model.

Install

  1. Open RecastInline.dmg and drag Recast Inline into Applications.
  2. Double-click to launch.
  3. Grant Accessibility permission when prompted.
