Recast Inline

Rephrase, fix, and reshape your writing in any app — with a single hotkey. Runs entirely on-device with Apple Intelligence. No accounts, no cloud, no data leaves your Mac.

Download for macOS

Free internal test build · ad-hoc signed · ~latest release

Requirements

  • macOS 26 or later.
  • An Apple Intelligence–capable Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on (the rephrasing runs on the built-in on-device model).

Install (first launch takes 2 extra steps)

This is an internal test build, so it isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet. macOS Gatekeeper will block the first launch and may say the app is "damaged" or from an "unidentified developer." That's expected — here's how to open it.

  1. Open the downloaded RecastInline.dmg and drag Recast Inline into your Applications folder.
  2. Clear the download flag so it will open. Pick one:
    • Terminal (most reliable): paste this and press Return — 
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/RecastInline.app
      then double-click the app.
    • No Terminal: try to open the app once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  3. When prompted, grant Accessibility permission (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility). This is required for the global hotkey to type the rephrased text back into your app.

Heads-up: because test builds are ad-hoc signed, you may need to re-grant Accessibility after installing a new version.

How to use it

  1. Select some text in any app.
  2. Press ⌥⌘R (or pick an action from the menu-bar R icon / the Services menu).
  3. Recast Inline rewrites your selection in place.
